## 4. Rule (frozen)

- Span: from `2024-07-01T00:00:00.000Z` to `2026-07-01T00:00:00.000Z` (exclusive). Decision times T: 2024-07-01 00:00 UTC, then every 8 hours, last 2026-06-30 16:00 UTC (2,190 times).
- Spread S(T) = (sum of Hyperliquid funding rates with event time in (T - 24h, T]) - (sum of Binance settlement rates with event time in (T - 24h, T]).
- Funding coverage at T: Hyperliquid has at least 22 events in (T - 24h, T], and Binance has at least (24 / intervalHours - 1) events in the same window, where intervalHours is the interval of its latest settlement at or before T. Without coverage S(T) is undefined and treated as 0 for the "previous" test.
- Entry at T for a coin that is tradable, not currently held, with |S(T)| >= 0.0020 (tolerance 1e-12) and either T is the first decision time or |S(T - 8h)| < 0.0020 (undefined counts as below). Sign +1 (S > 0) is short Hyperliquid, long Binance. Sign -1 is the reverse. Entry prices are the opens at T. A crossing that finds no free slot is lost (counted `noSlot`); a coin that stays above the level without crossing never enters. A crossing with prices missing is lost (counted `noPrice`). `noCoverage` is counted at a decision time when a coin that is not held has S(T) undefined, S(T - 8h) defined, and either T is the first decision time or |S(T - 8h)| < 0.0020. Nothing enters at the last decision time. A position closed at T frees its slot for entries at the same T.
- Order of entries at the same T: larger |S(T)| first, ties by Hyperliquid coin name ascending.
- Slots: 10 concurrent positions. Unit notional per leg at entry, each leg 1x margined on its own venue, capital 2 per unit per slot (20 for the book).
- Unavailability: at every decision time T after entry, if either price at T (Binance 1h bar or Hyperliquid 8h candle opening at T) is missing, the position closes at once at the opens of the last earlier decision time at which both prices existed, with funding counted up to that earlier time and the slot freed at T (reason `unavailable`). This takes precedence over every exit test at that T and applies whether or not an exit signal exists, with ONE exception: a liquidation of the short leg inside the step that ends at T (next bullet) is still applied. It covers a delisting, where funding stops and no signal could ever fire.
- Exit at the first T' after entry with sign x S(T') <= 0 (tolerance 1e-12) when S(T') is defined (an undefined S(T') gives no exit), at the opens of T', provided both prices at T' exist. At the last decision time every open position with both prices closes at the opens with reason `end`, even when an exit signal also fires there.
- Liquidation (checked for each 8h step (T - 8h, T] a position was held across, before the exit and unavailability tests at T). Each leg is margined 1x on its own venue with a maintenance margin of 5%. A 1x long leg is never liquidated before its price reaches zero, so only the SHORT leg is tested: it is liquidated when its venue's high over the step reaches `2 / 1.05` (1.9047619...) times its entry open or more. Binance uses the eight 1h bars opening T - 8h to T - 1h (those that exist), Hyperliquid the 8h candle opening T - 8h (if it exists). If it happens the position closes (reason `liquidated`) with the short leg's return replaced by -1.0 of unit notional. The long leg closes at the opens of T when both prices at T exist, otherwise at the opens of the last earlier decision time with both prices, with funding counted up to the same time. Costs are charged as for any exit. Candle highs stand in for the exchange mark price, a stated approximation.
- Funding is credited on the unit notional at entry; the drift of the notional with price during the hold is ignored (an approximation, stated). Funding rates missing during a hold count as zero (stated limitation).
- Capital accounting is fixed-exposure: every position has unit notional, the book never resizes, no equity or fee reserve is tracked, and the denominator 20 is the margin of ten full slots at 1x on two venues. The statistic is the P&L of a fixed-size book per unit of that capital.
- Execution convention: fills happen at the open of the bar at T, which assumes the settlement published at T is known at that bar's first trade. The same book is also reported with entry one decision time later (entry at the opens of T + 8h for the crossings found at T, no re-test, if tradable and a slot is free). Only the zero-delay book is judged.

## 5. Costs (judged profile)

Per side, both legs, taker: Binance 5 bps fee + 2 bps slippage, Hyperliquid 4.5 bps fee + 7 bps slippage, total 0.00185 a side, 0.0037 a round trip, charged at entry and at every exit including liquidation, `unavailable` and `end`. Sources: Binance VIP 0 taker 5 bps (owner's fills 2026-02), Hyperliquid base taker 4.5 bps (docs 2026-11-10), Hyperliquid slippage 7.06 bps is the median one-way over 179 coins for a 1,000 USD market order on 2026-11-11, the Binance slippage of 2 bps is an ASSUMPTION, unmeasured. Limitation: 2026 depth is applied to 2024-2026 trades, and in 2024 small-coin books were probably thinner. Reported but NOT judged: scout `taker` (0.00155 a side), `taker+10bps` (0.00355 a side), and the locked profile plus 5 bps a leg (0.00285 a side). Because the book does not depend on cost, those profiles re-price the same positions.

## 6. Statistics and hurdle

- Per-position net = sign x (sum of Hyperliquid minus Binance funding rates with event time in (entryT, exitT]) + basis - 2 x perSide, with basis = sign x ((B_exit / B_entry - 1) - (H_exit / H_entry - 1)) (B Binance open, H Hyperliquid open) or the liquidation rule above.
- Annual return on capital = sum of net of all positions / 20 / 2.0 (730 days = 2.0 years exactly).
- 95% interval: cluster bootstrap on the annual return itself. Clusters are the 105 seven-day blocks of the span from 2024-07-01 (cluster of a position = floor((entryT - 2024-07-01) / 7 days)), a block with no entries is an empty cluster. Each resample draws 105 clusters with replacement and recomputes sum of nets / 20 / 2.0. 10,000 resamples, generator mulberry32 seed 20261111, one draw `floor(rng() * 105)` per cluster in order, percentile method with linear interpolation, lower end = 2.5% quantile.
- PASS: annual return on capital >= 5.0% AND the lower end of the interval > 0. Anything else is FAIL.
- Also reported, not judged: by half-year and by year, slot use, number of distinct coins, counts of positions by exit reason, `noSlot`, `noPrice`, `noCoverage`, the worst position, the zero-delay against one-step-delay books, profiles in section 5.

## 7. Prediction (owner, registered before the read)

The annual return on capital is positive but below 7.75% a year (half of the scouted +15.5%), because the scout window was Hyperliquid's points season. The weekly entry clusters do not capture overlap of holds across weeks, so the interval may be too narrow (stated). The hurdle is 5%, so the prediction is compatible with a FAIL (0 to 5%) and with a narrow PASS (5% to 7.75%), but a point estimate above 5% can still FAIL on the interval. A result at or above 7.75% or at or below 0% contradicts the prediction.
